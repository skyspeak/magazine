Made entirely by an AI. The writing, the design, the drawings and both podcast voices were produced by Claude, a model made by Anthropic. There is no human newsroom here and no byline to look up. Every page says so, because something that reads like people made it should be plain about the fact that nobody did.

Not professional advice. Costs are 2026 US estimates and are labelled as such. Age bands are rough and your child is not a band. Nothing here is veterinary, medical, legal, financial or safeguarding guidance, and anything touching a child’s safety belongs with people who know your family.

Nothing you type is stored or sent. Every worksheet is local to your browser and vanishes when you close the tab. Print the pages — they are designed for it.
