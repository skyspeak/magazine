So you want to stay home alone

What they’re actually scared of, what to say, and the one job that does the work.

The short version

They’re scared ofThe doorbell. And you not picking up.
SayGive me twenty minutes while you get milk.
Don’t say“I’m not a baby.” True. Still annoying.
Your jobAnswer your phone. Every time. Miss it once and this gets very slow.
If they say not yetAsk for ten minutes while they’re in the garden.

Why that works

They’re not picturing burglars. They’re picturing you not answering your phone and them driving home imagining things.

So the whole job is: be reachable and be boring. Ask for twenty minutes, not an afternoon. Small and successful beats big and refused.

What to actually do

  1. Ask for twenty minutes while they do something local. Not a whole afternoon.
  2. Tell them your plan for the door, the smoke alarm, and getting locked out - before they ask you.
  3. Answer your phone every single time. This is the entire test and it is easy to pass.

These four are what actually happens. Not burglars.

For the grown-up reading over a shoulder

This is the child’s half of Issue No. 07. Nothing here contradicts yours. It tells them that asking repeatedly fails, that you are worried about something specific and findable, and that “not yet” is a real answer if it comes with a date. If you are going to say not yet, please bring the date.
