So you want to change how you look

What they’re actually scared of, what to say, and the one job that does the work.

The short version

They’re scared ofOnly the permanent ones. That’s genuinely it.
SayThis one washes out. Can that one be mine?
Don’t say“It’s my body.” True. Still loses the argument.
Your jobSplit your list into grows-back and doesn’t. Ask for the grows-back ones first.
If they say not yetGet a date for the permanent one. Then drop it until then.

Why that works

Grown-ups mostly panic about this because they picture the permanent version. If you show them you already know the difference, you’ve done most of the work.

So lead with ‘this washes out’. It sounds like you’ve thought about it, because you have, and it’s very hard to argue with.

What to actually do

  1. Sort your list yourself first: grows back, washes out, or permanent.
  2. Ask for the reversible ones. Say the word ‘reversible’ - it does a lot of work.
  3. For a permanent one, ask for a date instead of a yes. Then genuinely don’t mention it again until then.

Which of yours grow back?

Sort them. Then ask for the first two columns.

For the grown-up reading over a shoulder

This is the child’s half of Issue No. 10. Nothing here contradicts yours. It tells them that asking repeatedly fails, that you are worried about something specific and findable, and that “not yet” is a real answer if it comes with a date. If you are going to say not yet, please bring the date.
