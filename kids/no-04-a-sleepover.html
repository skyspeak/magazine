So you want a sleepover

What they’re actually scared of, what to say, and the one job that does the work.

The short version

They’re scared ofYou at 1am, in a house they’ve never been in.
SayIf I want to come home I’ll text you. I won’t be embarrassed.
Don’t say“I’ll be fine!” Then at 1am you’re stuck being fine.
Your jobPick a code word together. Boring. Works.
If they say not yetAsk for a late-over. All the fun, home at nine.

Why that works

Here’s a thing almost nobody tells you: loads of people want to go home at 1am on their first sleepover. It is completely normal and it is not being a baby.

The reason it goes badly is that people decide it would be embarrassing to say so, and then lie awake until six. A code word fixes that. You text one word, they come, nobody says anything about it.

What to actually do

  1. Agree the code word before you go. Any word. ‘Bananas’ works.
  2. Take your own toothbrush and something that smells like home. Sounds childish; helps at 1am.
  3. If you want to go home, send the word. That’s it. You never have to say it out loud to anyone.
