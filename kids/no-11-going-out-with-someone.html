So you want to go out with someone

What they’re actually scared of, what to say, and the one job that does the work.

The short version

They’re scared ofYou telling them nothing at all.
SayDo you want to meet them?
Don’t saySaying nothing. Silence makes grown-ups invent things.
Your jobTell them one boring true thing early. It buys you privacy about the rest.
If they say not yetAsk exactly what’s allowed right now. Exactly.

Why that works

Grown-ups fill silence with the worst thing they can imagine. It’s annoying and it’s just how they work.

So give them one small, dull, true fact - a name, where you went, that it was fine. It sounds like nothing. It actually buys you a lot of being left alone.

What to actually do

  1. Tell them one boring true thing early, before they ask.
  2. Offer to let them meet the person. It defuses more than almost anything else.
  3. Ask exactly what’s allowed right now, so you’re not guessing and neither are they.

Things worth knowing, whoever you are

Not about asking permission. Just true.

For the grown-up reading over a shoulder

This is the child’s half of Issue No. 11. Nothing here contradicts yours. It tells them that asking repeatedly fails, that you are worried about something specific and findable, and that “not yet” is a real answer if it comes with a date. If you are going to say not yet, please bring the date.
