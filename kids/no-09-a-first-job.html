So you want a job

What they’re actually scared of, what to say, and the one job that does the work.

The short version

They’re scared ofYour sleep.
SayCap my hours, and check my grades in a month.
Don’t say“It won’t affect anything.” It will. Say so first.
Your jobMake the phone calls yourself. Including the awkward one.
If they say not yetAsk to do one paid thing first. Babysitting. Someone’s garden.

Why that works

Offering the cap yourself is the move here. If you say ‘cap me at ten hours and check my grades in a month’, you’ve just made their argument for them and there’s nothing left to refuse.

The bit that will actually be hard is ringing a manager to say you can’t work Saturday. Do it anyway. That phone call is the most useful thing about the whole job.

What to actually do

  1. Suggest the hours cap before they do. Ten is a good number to offer.
  2. Offer a check-in after a month, with your actual grades. It sounds confident because it is.
  3. Make every work phone call yourself, even the awkward ones. Especially those.

Can you do these yet?

Be honest - these are the job, more than the work is.

For the grown-up reading over a shoulder

This is the child’s half of Issue No. 09. Nothing here contradicts yours. It tells them that asking repeatedly fails, that you are worried about something specific and findable, and that “not yet” is a real answer if it comes with a date. If you are going to say not yet, please bring the date.
